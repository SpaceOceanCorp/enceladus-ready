        What is real here and what is made up
        • Real: the ground heights and slopes (Schenk & McKinnon 2024 DEM, points about 390 m apart); the tiger stripes (a line drawn through each stripe's jet sources from Porco et al. 2014, so it is approximate); where Earth is in the sky, its rising and setting, Saturn hiding it once every 33-hour orbit, and the 71–84 minute one-way signal delay (JPL Horizons, Nov 2026 to Dec 2027).
        • Assumed or made up: the rover itself (no Enceladus rover is planned); its speed of 120 m/h, its 25° slope limit (Perseverance is rated for 30°), and its power (a 110 W RTG and a 2.4 kWh battery, borrowed from Perseverance); a 2 km no-go band along each stripe; slower driving within 1.5 km of a jet; the science-interest score; an antenna horizon of 5° with no hills blocking the view; Earth-side antennas always available.
        • The route planner and the rover picking its own targets follow ideas from space-mission-control-center by ajmcastro (MIT licence).

        Life detection

        NASA's proposed Enceladus Orbilander would land and test plume fallout for signs of life. Here you run its proposed Life Detection Suite. The model and every number come from sisl/LifeDetection (Stanford SISL with NASA Ames; Kim et al. 2025).

          Where every number comes from
          • Chance of life before any reading (10%), and how likely each reading is with and without life: src/bayes_net.jl. Curves are cut into bins as in src/common/bayes_net_helpers.jl.
          • The six instruments, what each measures, the sample each uses, the 500-unit tank and fallout of about 270 units per collection (±25%): src/LifeDetectionPOMDP.jl. Units are the repo's scaled sample units.
          • The mission baseline (ConOps) and the 99.9% / 0.1% thresholds for declaring: conops_orbiter and simulate_policyVLD in src/common/simulate.jl.
          • Different from the repo: whether life is present stays fixed for the whole landing (the repo redraws it with each new batch of fallout), and the one-step lookahead is ours. The paper's planner (SARSOP) is not run here.
          • The probabilities are the authors' modelling assumptions, not measurements. The repo has no licence, so its code is not copied; the model is rebuilt here from its published numbers.
